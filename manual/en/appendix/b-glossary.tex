\section{Modelling concepts}

\begin{longtable}{@{}p{0.16\linewidth}p{0.24\linewidth}p{0.52\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule \textbf{Chinese} & \textbf{English} & \textbf{Notes} \\ \midrule \endhead
参数化建模 & parametric modelling & Describes a model through dimensions, constraints, and feature history; change a parameter and the model changes \\
草图 & sketch & A 2D profile, usually the cross-section of a 3D feature \\
约束 & constraint & A rule that fixes a geometric relationship (horizontal, tangent, distance, ...) \\
自由度 & degree of freedom (DoF) & The number of independent variables in a sketch that are not yet constrained \\
完全约束 & fully constrained & Zero degrees of freedom; the shape is uniquely determined \\
构造几何 & construction geometry & Lines that only help you draw and do not form the shape \\
尺寸 & dimension & A numeric constraint (length, angle, radius, ...) \\
参考（从动） & reference / driven & A dimension that only measures and does not drive \\
凸台 & Pad & Extrudes a closed profile along its normal into a solid \\
凹槽 & Pocket & Cuts a recess or through-hole in a solid using a profile \\
旋转 & Revolution & Revolves a profile around an axis into a solid of revolution \\
槽 & Groove & Cuts an annular groove in a solid of revolution \\
放样 & Loft & Blends several cross-sections into a solid \\
管道 / 扫掠 & Pipe / Sweep & Sweeps a profile along a path \\
螺旋 & Helix & Sweeps along a helix (springs, threads) \\
圆角 & Fillet & Turns a sharp edge into a rounded transition \\
倒角 & Chamfer & Cuts a bevel on a sharp edge \\
拔模 & Draft & Adds a draft angle to the side faces \\
厚度 & Thickness & Hollows a solid into a thin wall \\
孔 & Hole & Standard hole feature (can include threads and counterbores) \\
阵列 & Pattern & Linear, polar, path, or point pattern \\
镜像 & Mirrored & Mirrors about a plane \\
实体（体） & Body & The PartDesign container that holds the feature history \\
特征 & feature & One step in the modelling history (pad, fillet, ...)\\
Tip & Tip & The marker for how far the body is computed \\
基准面/轴/点 & Datum plane/axis/point & Reference geometry that helps you model \\
附着 & Attachment & Attaches a sketch or datum geometry to a face, edge, or coordinate system \\
布尔运算 & boolean operation & Union, difference, intersection \\
B-rep & boundary representation & A geometric representation that describes a solid by its bounding surfaces \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\section{Assembly, drawings, and simulation}

\begin{longtable}{@{}p{0.16\linewidth}p{0.24\linewidth}p{0.52\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule \textbf{Chinese} & \textbf{English} & \textbf{Notes} \\ \midrule \endhead
装配 & Assembly & The combination and constraint of several components \\
构件 & component & A part or sub-assembly within an assembly \\
关节 / 配合 & joint & A relationship that limits the relative motion of two components \\
接地 & grounded & Fixes a component in space \\
求解 & solve & Computes the degrees of freedom and positions of the assembly \\
工程图 & drawing (TechDraw) & A 2D sheet generated from the 3D model \\
投影组 & projection group & A group of views such as front, top, and right \\
剖视图 & section view & A view cut open by a section plane \\
局部详图 & detail view & An enlarged view of a local area \\
尺寸标注 & dimension & A dimension on a drawing \\
电子表格 & spreadsheet & A parameter table that expressions can reference \\
别名 & alias & A cell's name, such as \file{Thickness} \\
表达式 & expression & A formula starting with \file{=} \\
单位制 & unit system & Millimetres/metres, inches, and so on \\
有限元 & FEM & Finite element analysis \\
网格划分 & meshing & Divides a solid into elements \\
边界条件 & boundary condition & Fixed supports, loads, and so on \\
求解器 & solver & CalculiX (ccx) and others \\
后处理 & post-processing & Contour plots, deformation, result export \\
刀路 & toolpath & The machining path generated by CAM \\
后处理器 & post-processor & Turns a toolpath into machine G-code \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\section{Files and technology}

\begin{longtable}{@{}p{0.16\linewidth}p{0.24\linewidth}p{0.52\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule \textbf{Chinese} & \textbf{English} & \textbf{Notes} \\ \midrule \endhead
FCStd & FreeCAD document & Native document that keeps the modelling history \\
STEP & STEP (.step/.stp) & A general solid interchange format \\
IGES & IGES (.iges/.igs) & An older surface/wireframe interchange format \\
STL & STL (.stl) & Triangle mesh, common for 3D printing \\
OBJ & OBJ (.obj) & Mesh format with materials and textures \\
3MF & 3MF (.3mf) & A modern 3D printing format \\
DXF / DWG & DXF / DWG & 2D CAD interchange formats \\
SVG & SVG & Vector graphics (drawings or imported artwork)\\
PDF & PDF & Drawing output and printing \\
IFC & IFC & Building information model interchange format \\
G-code & G-code & Numerical control machine instructions \\
SAF & Storage Access Framework & Android's system file picker \\
手势 & gesture & Touch-screen operation \\
精密指针 & precision pointer & The crosshair that appears above your finger on a long-press \\
导航立方体 & NaviCube & The orientation indicator and view switcher in the top-right corner \\
事务 & transaction & One unit of change that can be undone \\
恢复副本 & recovery copy & A copy of the document saved automatically after a crash \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
