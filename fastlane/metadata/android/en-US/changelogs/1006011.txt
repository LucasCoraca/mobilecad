• Large STEP files: the progress indicator shows how long the import has been running, so you can tell it is still working (a STEP assembly of thousands of parts can take several minutes to read, as on the desktop).
• Large models are drawn faster (the parts' triangles are prepared about 3× faster) and with the desktop's mesh fineness, so more of a large model fits in memory.
• If the app was closed while a very large model was open, it no longer reopens that model at every start: unsaved work is reopened once, and if the app closes again before it is changed or saved, it starts with an empty document.
